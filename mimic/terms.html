Cloning a voice: consent is the rule

Mimic can build a voice from a short recording. Whose voice you may record is not a technical question, and the app cannot check it for you, so it is stated plainly here:

  • Your own voice — always fine.
  • Somebody else's voice — only with their clear, informed and current permission, given by someone able to give it. Permission for one purpose is not permission for every purpose.
  • A child's voice — only if you are their parent or legal guardian, or have that person's permission.
  • A public figure, performer, or a voice from a recording you did not make — a voice being easy to obtain is not permission to clone it. Many jurisdictions protect a person's voice as part of their likeness, separately from any copyright in the recording.

Mimic asks you to acknowledge this once before it will record a voice sample. That acknowledgement is a reminder, not a transfer of responsibility: you remain responsible for every voice you create and for what you do with it.
