Mimic

Voice cloning that never leaves your iPhone.

Mimic is a voice cloning and real-time voice changing app for iPhone. Clone a voice from a short sample, then perform with it live — entirely on your device.

What it does

Performer Mode — live

Speak into your iPhone with headphones on. Mimic transforms your voice in about a second and pipes it back to your ears, so you can perform in another voice in real time. Powered by the Neural Engine — no internet required.

Studio cloning, with permission

Read a short script and Mimic builds a voice avatar from it. Then perform with it live, or convert a recording. Clone your own voice, or someone else's with their clear consent — Mimic asks you to confirm that before you record a sample.

Built-in voice library

Fourteen voices included out of the box. Six free effects — Robot, Helium, Demon, Underwater, Whisper and Echo — work on any supported iPhone. Eight designer voices, from Princess and Super Hero for the kids to character voices for creators and performers, unlock with Mimic Pro.

Private by design

Voice cloning runs entirely on your iPhone's Neural Engine. Recordings, voice embeddings, and converted audio never leave the device. Mimic has no servers, no analytics, no third-party SDKs — there is nowhere for your voice to go.

Common questions

Can I clone a voice on an iPhone?

Yes. Mimic records a short voice sample — around 45 seconds of clean speech — and builds a voice avatar from it directly on the phone. No upload, no account, and no waiting on a server to process it. You can then use that avatar to convert a recording or to perform live.

For the full walkthrough — how to record a sample that actually works, what the hardware needs to be, and the consent rules that apply — see the guide to cloning a voice on an iPhone.

Is there an offline voice cloning app for iPhone?

Mimic is built for exactly that. The speaker embedding, the voice conversion and the audio playback all run on-device using Core ML on Apple's Neural Engine, so it works in airplane mode. Most voice cloning tools send your audio to a server; Mimic has no backend at all.

Can it change my voice in real time?

Yes — that is Performer Mode. With headphones or AirPods connected, you speak and hear yourself transformed roughly a second later, continuously. It also works through a Bluetooth speaker for performing to a room.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Voice cloning needs the Neural Engine on iPhone 15 Pro or newer. The six built-in voice effects — Robot, Helium, Demon, Underwater, Whisper and Echo — run on any supported iPhone, since they use classic audio processing rather than a machine-learning model.

Is voice cloning legal, and what is it for?

Mimic is a creative tool: voicing characters, narration and dubbing for your own videos, performing, and playing with voices with your kids. Clone your own voice freely, and anyone else's only with their clear permission. Using a cloned voice to impersonate someone without consent, to deceive, or to harass is against Mimic's terms and is likely illegal where you live. The app asks you to acknowledge this before you record a voice sample.

What does it cost?

Free to download, including the six built-in voice effects, Performer Mode and recordings. Mimic Pro unlocks voice cloning and the eight designer voices, as either a monthly subscription — which starts with a 7-day free trial — or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store and on the in-app upgrade screen.
