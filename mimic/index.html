Mimic

Sound like anyone. On your phone.

A live voice changer and voice cloning studio for iPhone. Everything stays on your device.

What it does

Performer Mode — live

Speak into your iPhone with headphones on. Mimic transforms your voice in about a second and pipes it back to your ears, so you can talk live as someone else. Powered by the Neural Engine — no internet required.

Studio cloning

Read a short script and Mimic builds an avatar of that voice. Then drive it live, or convert any recording. Clone your own voice, a family member's, a character — anything you have ten seconds of clean audio for.

Built-in voice library

Nine voices included out of the box, from Princess and Super Hero for the kids to character voices for performers and prank-callers. Three voices are free; the rest unlock with Mimic Pro.

Private by design

Voice cloning runs entirely on your iPhone's Neural Engine. Recordings, voice embeddings, and converted audio never leave the device. Mimic has no servers, no analytics, no third-party SDKs — there is nowhere for your voice to go.

Your voice never leaves your phone.

Every step of the pipeline — speaker embedding, voice conversion, audio playback — runs on-device using Core ML on Apple's Neural Engine. Mimic ships without a backend, because it doesn't need one.
