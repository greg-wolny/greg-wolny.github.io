Refract

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-31. Last updated 2026-08-31.

Refract does not collect your data. Your music, your photos and the videos you make stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and Refract runs no servers that see your work.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Refract and everything it makes: the audio you import, the photographs you add, the projects you build, and the videos you export. Projects live in Refract's own folder on your iPhone and are included in your device backup if you have one enabled. Deleting the app deletes them.

How your music is analyzed

Refract reads your track on your device to find its beats, tempo and structure, and to measure the frequencies it draws from. That analysis happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's own audio frameworks. The audio is never uploaded, never streamed to us, and never sent to any third party.

What we do receive

Only what you deliberately send:

  • Feedback. If you use Send feedback in Settings, we receive your message, the category you picked, your app version, your iOS version, your device model, your system locale, and whether you are on the free tier, a trial or Refract Pro. That list is shown to you on the feedback screen before you send. No projects, no photos, no audio and no exports are attached.

Feedback reaches us by email and is used only to answer you and to fix what you reported. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Purchases

Refract Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Refract asks Apple's StoreKit whether the current Apple Account is entitled to Pro, and that answer stays on your device.

Photo library access

When you add a photograph, iOS shows you its own picker and Refract receives only the pictures you choose. If you take a photo inside Refract, the camera is used for that one shot. Refract does not browse your library and does not read your location, faces or albums.

Sharing an export

When you share a finished video, iOS hands it to whichever app you pick — Instagram, TikTok, Messages, Files, or anything else. From that point their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Children

Refract is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any material change will be described in the app's release notes.
