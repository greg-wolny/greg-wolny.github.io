Frequently asked

What does Verdict do?

Verdict analyzes a photo and estimates whether it was generated or manipulated by AI. It runs four independent checks — a neural-network model, sensor-noise analysis, Error Level Analysis, and metadata inspection — and combines them into a single 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict, from "Likely Real" to "Likely AI-Generated," plus a per-check breakdown.

How accurate is it? Is the verdict definitive?

No AI detector is perfect, and Verdict is no exception. AI image generators improve constantly, and both false positives (real photos flagged as AI) and false negatives (AI images that slip through) are possible. Treat Verdict's score as one strong piece of evidence to weigh with your own judgment — not as absolute proof. That's why Verdict always shows the breakdown behind its score instead of a single yes/no.

Does Verdict work without an internet connection?

Yes. All analysis runs on-device, so Verdict works fully offline when you analyze a photo from your library. The one exception is sharing a web link: if you share a URL instead of an image file, Verdict downloads the image at that address before analyzing it on-device.

How do I check an image?

Open Verdict and tap "Select Image to Analyze" to pick a photo, or use the share sheet: in Photos, Safari, or Messages, tap the Share button on an image and choose Verdict. Results appear in a few seconds.

How do I add Verdict to my share sheet?

Share any image, scroll the app row to the right, tap "More," then "Edit," and turn on Verdict (or drag it up). After that, Verdict appears in the share sheet whenever you share an image.

Which iPhones can run Verdict?

Verdict runs on iPhones with iOS 16 or later. Analysis is a little faster on newer devices, but it works across the supported range.

Are my photos uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Images are analyzed on your device and discarded when you're done. Verdict keeps no library or history of what you scan, runs no servers, and includes no analytics. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Why did Verdict get this image wrong?

A few things can throw off any detector: heavy compression or re-saving, screenshots, small or low-resolution images, and AI images that have been edited to look photographic. If you hit a case where Verdict is confidently wrong, email us the details (and the image if you can) — it helps us improve the scoring.

How much does Verdict cost?

Verdict is free to download and use.
