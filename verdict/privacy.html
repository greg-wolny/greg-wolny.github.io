Verdict

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-07. Last updated 2026-07-07.

Verdict analyzes your images on your iPhone. We do not run servers, we do not collect analytics, and we do not include any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. Your photos are not uploaded to us.

What stays on your device

Verdict is a photo-analysis tool. When you analyze an image, everything happens locally on your iPhone:

  • The image you check — read into memory, analyzed on-device, and discarded when you're done. Verdict does not maintain a library or history of the images you scan.
  • The AI-detection model — a neural network bundled inside the app. Inference runs locally through Core ML and ONNX Runtime. No image is uploaded for processing.
  • The forensic checks — noise analysis, Error Level Analysis, and metadata (EXIF) inspection all run in the app, on your device.

The share extension

Verdict includes a share-sheet extension so you can check an image directly from Photos, Safari, or Messages. When you share an image file, the extension hands that image to the Verdict app on your device for analysis — nothing is sent anywhere else.

If you share a web link rather than an image file, the extension downloads the image at that address so it can be analyzed on-device. That request goes to the website hosting the image (the same as opening the link in a browser); it does not route through any Verdict server, and none of your data is attached to it.

Permissions Verdict may request

Verdict is deliberately minimal. It uses Apple's system photo picker, which lets you choose a single image without granting access to your whole photo library. Verdict does not request Microphone, Location, Contacts, Calendar, HealthKit, or Photo Library write access.

What Verdict does not do

  • We do not operate any servers or backends. Verdict ships without one.
  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs for advertising, tracking, attribution, crash reporting, or social media.
  • We do not upload, transmit, store, or share the images you analyze.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any data, because we have no data to sell.

On-device machine learning

Verdict's AI-detection model is a Swin Transformer image classifier that runs locally on your iPhone through ONNX Runtime. It does not talk to the network, and no analysis result leaves the device unless you choose to share it yourself.

Accuracy and intended use

Verdict is a detection aid, not a guarantee of authenticity. AI image generators evolve quickly and no detector — including Verdict — is correct 100% of the time. Treat Verdict's score as one strong signal to weigh alongside your own judgment, not as definitive proof that an image is real or fake.

Purchases

Verdict is free. If a future version introduces optional paid features, they would be sold through Apple's StoreKit; Apple would process any transaction and Verdict would receive only the entitlement status, never your payment details. This page would be updated before any such change takes effect.

Children's privacy

Verdict is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because Verdict does not collect personal information from anyone, this is largely moot.

Changes to this policy

If Verdict ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page, bump the effective date above, and surface the change inside the app before it takes effect.
