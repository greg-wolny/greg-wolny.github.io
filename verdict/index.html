Verdict

Real photo, or AI?

Verdict tells you whether an image was generated or manipulated by AI — in seconds, entirely on your iPhone.

What it does

Four checks, one score

Verdict runs four independent forensic analyzers on every image — a neural-network model, sensor-noise analysis, Error Level Analysis, and metadata inspection — then combines them into a single 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict.

Check from anywhere

Pick a photo from your library, or tap Share on any image in Photos, Safari, or Messages and choose Verdict. The result comes back in seconds, with a breakdown of what each check found.

Private by design

Analysis runs entirely on your device using Core ML and ONNX Runtime. Your images are never uploaded to a server, never stored by us, and never shared. Verdict has no account system and no analytics.

Honest results

AI generators change fast, and no detector is perfect. Verdict shows you the evidence behind its score so you can weigh it with your own judgment — a strong signal, not a rubber stamp.

Your photos never leave your phone.

Every analyzer — the AI model, noise, ELA, and metadata checks — runs on-device. Verdict ships without a backend, because it doesn't need one.

No analytics. No third-party SDKs. No tracking. Read the policy.
