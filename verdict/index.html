Verdict

Real, or AI?

Verdict tells you whether a photo or video was generated by AI — in seconds, entirely on your iPhone.

What it does

Photos and video

For a photo, Verdict runs four independent forensic analyzers — a neural-network model, sensor-noise analysis, Error Level Analysis, and metadata inspection. For a video, it samples frames across the clip and runs the AI-detection model on each one, then combines the evidence into a single 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict.

Check from anywhere

Pick a photo or a video from your library, or tap Share on any image in Photos, Safari, or Messages and choose Verdict. The result comes back in seconds — for video, with honest frame-by-frame progress — alongside a breakdown of what each check found.

Private by design

Analysis runs entirely on your device using ONNX Runtime. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to a server, never stored by us, and never shared. Verdict has no account system and no analytics.

Honest results

AI generators change fast, and no detector is perfect. Verdict shows you the evidence behind its score so you can weigh it with your own judgment — a strong signal, not a rubber stamp.

Your photos and videos never leave your phone.

Every analyzer — the AI model, noise, ELA, and metadata checks, and the per-frame video analysis — runs on-device. Verdict ships without a backend, because it doesn't need one.
