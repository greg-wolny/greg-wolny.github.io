Verdict

Real, or AI?

Verdict is an AI image and video detector for iPhone that tells you whether a photo or video is real or AI-generated — in seconds, entirely on your device.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Photos and video

For a photo, Verdict scores three signals — a neural-network model, metadata inspection and sensor-noise analysis — weighted by how much each is actually worth. A fourth check, Error Level Analysis, is shown in the breakdown for reference but deliberately carries no weight, because it measures compression history rather than synthesis. For a video, Verdict samples 15 frames across the clip, runs the AI-detection model on each one, and takes the median. Either way you get a single 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict.

Check from anywhere

Pick a photo or a video from your library, or tap Share on any image in Photos, Safari, or Messages and choose Verdict. The result comes back in seconds — for video, with honest frame-by-frame progress — alongside a breakdown of what each check found.

Private by design

Analysis runs entirely on your device using ONNX Runtime. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to a server, never stored by us, and never shared. Verdict has no account system and no analytics.

Honest results

AI generators change fast, and no detector is perfect. Verdict shows you the evidence behind its score so you can weigh it with your own judgment — a strong signal, not a rubber stamp.

Common questions

How can I tell if a photo is AI-generated?

Open Verdict on your iPhone, pick the photo from your library or share it into the app, and it returns a 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict. The score combines three checks: a neural-network model trained to recognise generated imagery, inspection of the file's metadata for camera make, model, lens and exposure data, and sensor-noise analysis. Error Level Analysis is shown in the breakdown for reference but carries no weight in the score, because it measures compression history rather than synthesis.

You can also do a lot of this by hand, without any app — see the full guide to how to tell if an image is AI-generated.

Is there an iPhone app that detects AI-generated images?

Yes — Verdict. It checks both photos and video, runs entirely on the iPhone with no account and no uploads, and is free to download with five free checks to try it. Verdict Pro adds unlimited checks and unlocks the full per-check breakdown, and starts with a 7-day free trial.

Can it check videos too?

Yes. Verdict samples frames across the clip, runs the AI-detection model on each sampled frame, and combines the per-frame results into one verdict. You see real frame-by-frame progress while it works, and the per-frame evidence afterwards.

How accurate is it?

Verdict returns a probability, not a certainty. It is strongest on clearly synthetic imagery and on unmodified camera originals. Heavily processed phone photos — Night mode, Smart HDR, aggressive editing — and screenshots or re-compressed images carry less forensic signal, so borderline scores are more common there. That is why Verdict always shows the score and the evidence rather than a bare label.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every check runs on device, so Verdict works in airplane mode. It has no backend, which is also why nothing you scan is ever transmitted or stored anywhere.
