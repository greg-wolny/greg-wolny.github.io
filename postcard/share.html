What is Postcard?

Postcard is a private voice journal for iPhone and Apple Watch. Press record, speak a thought, and Postcard transcribes, summarizes, and indexes it — all on your device.

Sharing is optional, by design. When a friend sends you a postcard, only what they chose to include comes along — no servers, no tracking. Apple delivers the file through iMessage; Postcard runs no infrastructure of its own.
