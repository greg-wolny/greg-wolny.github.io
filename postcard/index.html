Postcard

The journal that knows when to ask.

Postcard is a private voice journal for iPhone and Apple Watch that you can ask questions. Speak a thought — it transcribes it, remembers it, and answers questions about your own moments.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Capture in seconds

Tap once on the wrist or phone. Postcard records, transcribes, and writes a one-line caption — all on-device. No accounts, no syncing, nothing leaves your phone.

Find what you said, by meaning

Search by what you meant, not just what you typed. Postcard understands "the offsite plan" and "Sarah's redesign" the same way your brain does.

Ask your own moments

"What did I learn this week?" Postcard answers from your own recordings, with the source moments cited and one tap away. Powered by Apple Intelligence on your phone.

Apple Watch first

Capture from your wrist with the Action Button, Double-Tap, or a complication. The Watch nudges you after a workout to capture a thought while it's still fresh.

Send to people you love

Pick a moment, choose what to include — voice, photo, place, transcript — and send it as a postcard via iMessage. Recipients with Postcard see it land in their timeline; everyone else gets a friendly nudge to install. No servers, no accounts, sender controls every byte.

Common questions

What is a voice journal?

Instead of typing an entry, you speak it. Postcard records the thought, transcribes it on-device, captions it, and files it on a timeline you can search and ask questions of later. It suits people who think out loud, or who want to journal while walking, driving or between meetings — moments when typing isn't practical.

Is there a journal app that remembers what I tell it?

That is the core of Postcard. Every captured moment is transcribed and indexed by meaning, so you can later ask "what did I decide about the kitchen?" or "what did I learn this week?" and get an answer drawn from your own recordings, with the original moments cited and one tap away.

Is it a better alternative to Voice Memos?

It solves a different problem. Voice Memos stores audio files you have to scrub through. Postcard transcribes each recording, captions it, places it on a timeline with location and photos, makes it searchable by meaning, and lets you ask questions across everything you have said.

Does it work offline, and is it private?

Yes to both. Recording, transcription, captioning, semantic search and the question answering all run on-device using Apple's Speech, FoundationModels and NaturalLanguage frameworks. Postcard has no servers and no account, so there is nowhere for your entries to be sent.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

The Watch is a first-class capture device, not an afterthought. Record with the Action Button, Double-Tap or a complication, and the Watch will prompt you after a workout to capture a thought while it is fresh. Entries sync to the iPhone when it is nearby.

What does it cost?

Free to download and use for capturing, transcribing and browsing your moments. Postcard Pro unlocks the full ask-your-moments experience and further capture features, as either a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store and on the in-app upgrade screen.

Private because it can be.
